Brake Systems, Shocks & Chassis Service

Ensure uncompromising stopping distance and road control with precision ceramic brake installations, rotor resurfacing, and heavy-duty suspension rebuilding.

Safety First

Zero Compromise When It Comes to Stopping Distance

Braking systems operate under extreme thermal friction. Worn friction pads or warped rotors dramatically extend stopping distance, compromise ABS pulsing during rain and snow, and cause severe pedal pulsation.

At Dan’s American Tire, our brake service begins with a comprehensive digital caliper measurement of pad thickness and rotor runout. We install premium low-dust ceramic pads and OE-spec slotted or vented rotors to ensure whisper-quiet, fade-resistant stopping performance on Stark County roadways.

  • Ceramic & Semi-Metallic Pads: Formulated for low brake dust, zero squeal, and exceptional heat dissipation.
  • Precision Rotor Resurfacing & Replacement: Removing grooves and lateral runout to eliminate pedal shaking.
  • Brake Fluid Exchange: Flushing moisture-contaminated hydraulic fluid to restore firm, responsive pedal feel.
Suspension & Handling

Restore That Smooth Factory Ride Quality

If your car nose-dives when braking, rocks excessively over bumps, or sways nervously on highway exit ramps, your struts and shock absorbers have likely lost their hydraulic dampening seal.

Worn suspension doesn't just make your ride uncomfortable — it prevents your tires from staying firmly planted on the road surface, reducing braking traction and accelerating uneven tire cupping.
