Advanced Shop Technology

Touchless Mounting & High-Speed Road Force Balancing

An improperly balanced tire causes steering wheel vibration at highway speeds and rapid, irregular tread scalloping. At Dan’s American Tire, our state-of-the-art computerized balancers measure dynamic and static balance with gram-level precision.

  • Touchless Tire Changers: Protect expensive alloy rims and powder-coated wheels from cosmetic scratches or rim edge marring.
  • TPMS Sensor Calibration: Diagnostic programming and battery status testing for tire pressure monitoring systems on all domestic and foreign models.
  • Rubber Stem & Valve Core Replacements: Fresh valve stems with every tire install to eliminate slow rim leaks and pressure drops.
